LOGOS — square PNG/SVG/WebP, ideally with a transparent background.
  experience/<company>.png     company you've worked at
  education/<university>.png   university / college
  markets/<company>.png        companies / instruments you invest in
  ventures/<business>.png      businesses you operate
Reference a logo in the matching content file, e.g.:
  logo: { name: "IIT Madras", src: "/images/logos/education/iit-madras.png" }
With no logo (or a missing file), a clean text monogram is shown instead.
